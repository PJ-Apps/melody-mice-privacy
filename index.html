Privacy Policy

App Name: Melody Mice

Effective Date: 20 October 2026

1. Introduction

Welcome to Melody Mice. Melody Mice is an Android music player that allows users to browse and play audio files stored on their devices. This Privacy Policy explains how the application handles file access, notifications, and information associated with its features.

2. Audio Files and Storage Permissions

Melody Mice may request audio or media permissions to find, display, and play supported music files stored on your device.

The application also allows users to select files from internal storage through the device's file picker for supported custom features or modifications.

Audio files and their metadata may be processed to provide music playback and display information such as track titles, artists, album artwork, and other available details.

The application should access only the files and information necessary for the features you use. Whether files remain entirely on your device or are transmitted elsewhere depends on the application's actual implementation.

3. Notifications

Melody Mice may request notification permission to provide playback-related notifications and media controls where supported. You can manage notification permissions through your Android device settings.

Disabling notifications may affect notification-based playback controls or other related features.

4. Music Library and Playlists

The application provides a music library and playlist-related features. Users can browse songs and organise music into playlists. Depending on the implementation, playlist information, selected tracks, and preferences may be stored locally on the device.

The application may provide library filters, including options related to short tracks, WAV audio files, and longer tracks. These options help users organise and browse their music library.

5. Playback Features

Melody Mice includes playback settings such as a sleep timer, gapless playback, crossfade, crossfade duration, and an option controlling whether audio can play simultaneously with other apps.

These features are intended to control the music playback experience. Their availability may depend on the device and installed application version.

6. Information Collection

The information handled by Melody Mice depends on its actual implementation. The developer must verify whether the application collects personal information, device identifiers, usage analytics, crash reports, or other diagnostic information.

Any data collection performed by integrated third-party libraries must also be considered and disclosed accurately.

Information processed by the application may be used to:

  • Find and play supported audio files.
  • Display music information and album artwork.
  • Provide playlist and music library features.
  • Apply available library filters.
  • Support user-selected file features.
  • Maintain application functionality, if applicable.
